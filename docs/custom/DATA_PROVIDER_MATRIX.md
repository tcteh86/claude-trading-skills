# Data provider matrix

The status column describes repository integration or proposed use. No live entitlement, price, coverage, or licensing claim was verified in Phase 0. Confirm rights for the actual account, dataset, exchange and use before enabling an adapter.

| Source | Status and candidate use | Coverage and failure risks | Rights and access questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial Modeling Prep | Existing generated and legacy clients plus canaries. Candidate for US listing, price, financials, estimates, actions and macro inputs. | Plan/endpoint differences, estimate sparsity, quota, renamed/nullable fields, restatements, delisting and adjustment ambiguity. Verify SGX independently. | Confirm market entitlements, local caching, snapshots, derived outputs and any publication limits. Keep keys out of URLs in logs. [FMP terms](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service) |
| SEC EDGAR and US issuer IR | Existing research source. Candidate for filing corroboration and Company Facts/XBRL. | Ticker/CIK mapping, amendments, accession availability, tags, scales, offset and fiscal calendars. Company Facts alone may omit context for an as-of replay. | Confirm API fair access, user-agent and rate requirements. Assess reuse rights of issuer attachments individually. [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) |
| SGX disclosures and issuer IR | Proposed primary evidence for SGX equities and REITs. | Reconcile announcement time, reported SGD/foreign currency, local ticker, counter type, units, rights, special DPU and restated figures. Confirm historical and delisted coverage. | Review access, automation, storage and excerpt permissions. Separate filing access from price and reference-data entitlements. [SGX announcements](https://www.sgx.com/securities/company-announcements) |
| Licensed SGX market data or broker export | Proposed EOD, actions and security master. Start with permitted manual samples. | Verify settled/traded currency, suspensions, corporate actions, liquidity, historical aliases, venue-specific sessions and inactive listings. | Confirm personal/non-display use, export, retention, redistribution and ML/agent-processing rights. [SGX market data](https://www.sgx.com/market-data) |
| Yahoo Finance via yfinance | Existing optional market source. Candidate for exploratory sanity checks only after review. | Availability and terms can change. Adjustments, history, rate limits and SGX completeness require reconciliation. | Review Yahoo terms and account permissions separately from the library license. [Yahoo terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html) |
| Finviz | Existing screening and theme input, including optional Elite workflows. | US-focused with plan/endpoint differences. Coverage, freshness, classifications and scraping restrictions need confirmation. | Confirm automated access and export permissions. Restrict to authorized use. [Finviz terms](https://finviz.com/terms.ashx) |
| ETF issuers and index providers | Proposed authoritative holdings, factsheets, fees and methodology. | Holdings can be delayed, sampled, partial, overlap, or embed derivatives. Reconcile weights/date, fund/index distinction and share class. | Verify holdings retention, derived exposures and index/classification trademark and redistribution terms separately. |
| MAS/FRED or licensed FX feed | Proposed FX/macroeconomic evidence. FXMacroData calendar already has an adapter. | Fixings differ from execution prices. Timezone, base/quote inversion, vintage and third-party series rights vary. | Confirm attribution, use and downstream rights at series level. [MAS statistics](https://www.mas.gov.sg/statistics) · [FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/) |
| CoinGecko and Binance | Existing crypto regime sources. CoinGecko also has a contract canary. | Aggregate volumes and venue-specific funding differ. Verify history, UTC cutoff, pool/symbol collisions, quote risk and restricted regions. | Verify subscription, permitted API use, attribution, geographic limitations and retention/publication terms. [CoinGecko terms](https://www.coingecko.com/en/terms) · [Binance terms](https://www.binance.com/en/terms) |
| TraderMonty public CSV, official macro and public reports | Existing breadth/uptrend CSV contracts and manual supporting inputs. | CSVs may be revised, stale, incomplete, or specific to a universe. News/search is a discovery input. Preserve source and availability. | Public access alone grants no reuse rights. Record source-specific licensing. Keep raw snapshots private where permitted and use links for references. [CSV integrity notes](../dev/public-csv-integrity.md) |
| Alpaca and local portfolio exports | Existing connectivity checks and reconciliation workflow. Prefer approved local exports for the fork. | Sensitive holdings/account information, incomplete activity and incompatible snapshot or cash semantics. | Confirm account/export terms and redact diagnostics. Use read-only data with explicit user approval. [Repository privacy policy](../en/privacy.md) |

Before enabling a provider, document datasets, scopes, freshness and history SLA, known gaps, rate limits, allowed endpoints, budget, terms date, permitted purpose, retention, and transformation/export rights. Record the reviewed evidence and expiry. Use synthetic or explicitly rights-cleared fixtures in Git and CI. Where entitlements do not support a complete universe or point-in-time history, narrow the claim or block the ranking.
